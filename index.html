FAQ

Questions before you start.

Do I need to already be in the U.S.?

No. The service is designed to help people research housing before they arrive.

Do you guarantee that a property will still be available?

No. Rental listings can change quickly. Shortlists reflect information available at the time of the search.

Do you act as my real-estate agent?

This service is positioned as property research and relocation assistance. Regulated brokerage, leasing, negotiation and referral activities may require state-specific compliance.
