FAQ

Questions before you start.

Do I need to already be in the U.S.?

No. The service is designed to help people research housing before they arrive.

Do you guarantee that a property will still be available?

No. Rental listings can change quickly. A shortlist is research based on the information available at the time of the search.

Do you act as my real-estate agent?

This MVP is positioned as a property research and relocation-assistance service. Regulated brokerage, leasing, negotiation and referral activities may require state-specific licensing or other compliance.

Can you search a city that isn't listed?

The initial version focuses on selected cities. Additional cities can be added as demand is validated.
